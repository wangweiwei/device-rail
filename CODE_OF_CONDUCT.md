# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and productive community for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience, nationality, appearance,
ability, or technical preference.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while taking security, privacy, and safety concerns
  seriously.
- Respect contributor time, platform constraints, and different levels of
  experience.
- Protect private device data, credentials, and unpublished vulnerabilities.
- Accept maintainer decisions and disengage when asked to stop an interaction.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate
misgendering, sustained disruption, publishing private information, exposing
secrets or device data, and retaliation against good-faith reporters are not
acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues privately through the repository owner or GitHub's abuse
reporting tools. Do not include security vulnerability details in a conduct
report; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead. Maintainers may edit or
remove content, issue a warning, limit participation, temporarily suspend, or
permanently ban participants according to severity and context.

Enforcement decisions should protect affected people, minimize unnecessary
disclosure, and avoid conflicts of interest. This policy applies to repository
issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, community events, and official
project communication.
